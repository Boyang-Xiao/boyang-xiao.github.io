**Challenge.** Combining locomotion with liquid handling and physiological sensing at the millimeter scale.

**What I did.**

- Designed magnetic soft robots with multimodal locomotion (climbing, crawling, walking, and rolling) to reach confined anatomical regions.
- Integrated wireless magnetic pumping and valving for targeted liquid release, sampling, and liquid biopsy, coordinated with robot locomotion.
- Developed wireless magnetic soft sensors for in situ, spatiotemporal mucus-viscosity measurement in the gastrointestinal tract.
- Modeled robot deformation under magnetic fields with Cosserat rod and beam theory.

**Skills.** Soft robotics, wireless microfluidics, sensor development, mechanics modeling, fabrication.

**Publications.** {% cite xiao2025device xiao2024mucus xu2023climbing %}. Related patent application: U.S. 18/882,837, _Viscosity sensors and wireless small-scale soft robots_.
