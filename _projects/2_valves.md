**Challenge.** Delivering liquid on demand from a flexible implantable platform, and measuring how much was released.

**What I did.**

- Developed a soft robotic patch with multiple flexible magnetic valves that can be controlled simultaneously for multiplexed intravesical drug delivery.
- Modeled valve behavior and the magnetic field distribution to predict valve opening and closing thresholds.
- Integrated resonant-frequency sensing with a vector network analyzer to estimate the released volume and close the loop on drug release.
- Mentored a master's student on a related minimally invasive valve therapy.

**Validation.** Closed-loop controlled-release experiments in an ex vivo porcine bladder model, in collaboration with a urologist.

**Skills.** Soft device design, wireless sensing, closed-loop control, clinician collaboration.
